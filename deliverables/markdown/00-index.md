## Product statement

> We don't put a price on what people have. We make it exchangeable.

Skills-Ring is a coordination and settlement network for non-monetary value. It connects what people can give with what they need, supports direct and multi-party exchange, records concrete service commitments when exchange is asynchronous, and uses fulfillment history to improve future matching and control risk.
